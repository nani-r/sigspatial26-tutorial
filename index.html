ACM SIGSPATIAL 2026 Tutorial November 3–6, 2026 · Riverside, CA

Indigenous Knowledge and Geospatial Bias in the Era of Large Language Models

Nicole R. Schneider (UMD) · Nandini Ramachandran (UMD) · Kent O’Sullivan (Sydney) · Hanan Samet (UMD)

This tutorial examines geospatial and representation bias in large language models using Indigenous place names and oral knowledge traditions as a case study. We pair technical methods (fairness metrics, debiasing, embedding geometry, unlearning, and structured vs. vector retrieval) with Indigenous Data Sovereignty (IDS) and the CARE Principles.
